# Scenario and data guide

## Seed

36 properties: 12 UK (London, York, Cornwall), 12 Spain (Málaga, Valencia, Mallorca), 12 Italy (Rome, Florence, Lake Como). Names, addresses, owners, guests, staff, suppliers, conversations and costs are fictional. All email identifiers use example.test. No actual contact endpoint, bank, OTA or property access system exists.

The booking schedule spans one year before the fixed date and at least 90 days after it, including cancellations, unpaid balances, channels, arrival requests, same-day arrivals and completed stays. Historical monthly expenses support reports. Each active booking has exactly one synchronised turnover task. Readiness is separate from booking status.

Readiness requires completed cleaning, inspection sign-off, required room evidence and verification of all critical repairs. Missing evidence never implies completion. A failed inspection creates rework. Staff assignment uses country, service city, skills, availability and a maximum of four tasks per shift. Travel assumptions are limited to matching fictional service areas.

## 1. Rescue an arrival

Country selects Willow Townhouse, Casa Oliva or Casa Luminosa. Launch sets the demo clock to its fixed morning and opens the story with cleaning complete, missing bedroom inspection evidence, and an unverified critical heating issue.

1. Observe source blockers.
2. Prepare the repair quote.
3. Approve cost: the issue becomes Scheduled and the home stays Not ready.
4. Record actual simulated repair completion evidence: readiness remains blocked.
5. Independently verify repair: an expense is posted once; inspection remains outstanding.
6. Complete inspection checklist and bedroom evidence: the home becomes Ready.
7. Create a local reassuring guest message and an owner-update task. Inspect audit and finance sources.

## 2. Fill a quiet week

Find future unbooked nights, propose up to five bounded 8% reductions, approve after revalidation, and verify history. No confirmed stay amount changes. No guaranteed booking or immediate revenue uplift is invented. An applied batch can be undone only if no later rate or booking depends on it. The normal reservation form can illustrate a separate synthetic later demand event.

## 3. Resolve a duplicate bill

Detect matching supplier/reference records, request a finance exception decision, approve exclusion of the draft, and verify the statement. The original posted invoice remains; posted totals and cash remain unchanged. The UI never calls this cash recovered.

## 4. Protect a shift handover

Replay an urgent fictional night-shift conversation, classify/escalate it, create service work, prepare a Spanish reply and approve its local send. Approve any required repair cost separately, record repair evidence, independently verify the repair and complete the service handover. Prepare factual follow-up while leaving any outstanding inspection requirement visible. No historical rating change or real dispatch is claimed.

## 5. Turn opportunity into work

Inspect direct/OTA mix and illustrative acquisition fees, articulate the direct-follow-up hypothesis, create 30/60/90-day tasks, assign an operations owner, complete the first action and schedule measurement. Business outcomes remain a hypothesis until later measured; completing a task never adds revenue.

## Replay and reset

Pause, manual step and auto-play update the actual scenario state. Replay is an explicit new demonstration of that selected story using current shared data; it does not silently erase unrelated user edits. Settings or Demo Studio's full reset rebuilds the exact baseline for every scenario and country. Playback pauses on hidden tabs and reload.
